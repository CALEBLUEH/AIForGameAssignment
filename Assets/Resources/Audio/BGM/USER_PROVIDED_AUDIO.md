# User-provided BGM

These eight MP3 files were supplied by the project owner on 2026-09-24 from
`AIforGameSound/BGM`. The filenames identify them as Blue Archive soundtrack
tracks produced by MO2.

No redistribution licence or attribution text was supplied with the files.
Before publicly distributing the game or repository, the project owner should
confirm that the music may be redistributed and add any required attribution.

Runtime mapping:

- `MainMenu_LevelSelection.mp3`: Lobby and Level Selection
- `Preparation.mp3`: SandBox Preparation
- `CharacterStates.mp3`: Sandbox Character States
- `Credits.mp3`: Credits
- `Level1.mp3`: Gameplay Level 1 and Sandbox Gameplay
- `Level2.mp3`: Gameplay Level 2
- `Level3.mp3`: Gameplay Level 3
- `Victory.mp3`: delayed victory-result music

The empty `Skill SFX` source folder was not imported. Future voice clips can be
played through `SceneMusicDirector.PlaySfx`, which follows the existing master
volume and mute setting.
